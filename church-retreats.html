Church Retreat Accommodation

Planning a church retreat on the Kenyan Coast requires more than rooms. Coast Bookings helps coordinate accommodation, meal requirements and preferred areas for church groups visiting Mombasa, Diani, Nyali, Shanzu, Kilifi and Watamu.

Request a Group Quote

Retreat needs we help clarify

Church groups often need full-board meals, meeting space, quiet surroundings, transport notes, family-friendly rooming or leader rooms. Include these details in your request so the team can coordinate realistic options.

Coast Bookings does not provide instant booking. Every church retreat request receives a personalized quotation based on your group size and dates.

Popular retreat destinations

  • Diani for a premium coastal retreat setting.
  • Shanzu for a calmer North Coast environment.
  • Mombasa or Nyali for easier movement.
  • Kilifi for quieter retreat-style programs.

Church retreat FAQ

Can we request full-board meals?

Yes. Full board and special diet notes can be included in your quote request.

Can you help with large church groups?

Yes. Share your guest count, dates and preferred destination so the team can coordinate suitable options.
