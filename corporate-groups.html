Plan the working parts of a company retreat

A team retreat and a working workshop need different accommodation arrangements. State how much of each day is reserved for work before comparing options: a lower room rate is not necessarily the lowest total event cost.

Include these details in your enquiry

  • Meeting dates, session times and whether the room must remain available between sessions.
  • Seating layout, projector or screen, internet requirements and power needs; ask for each item to be confirmed.
  • A rooming list distinguishing shared rooms, single rooms and facilitators, plus the person approving the quotation.
  • Meal and tea-break times, dietary requirements and any early departures.

Before accepting a quotation

Ask the quotation to separate accommodation, meals, meeting-room hire, equipment and transfers. Check whether taxes and any additional event charges are included before requesting internal approval.
