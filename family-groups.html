Build a rooming plan for your family group

The number of rooms matters as much as the total guest count. List adults and children separately, along with room-sharing preferences, so the requested options can be compared on the same basis.

Include these details in your enquiry

  • Numbers of adults and children, with children’s ages where a property needs them to confirm occupancy and meals.
  • Rooms that should be near each other, accessibility needs and any request for ground-floor rooms.
  • Arrival and departure dates for each household if relatives are not staying for the same nights.
  • Preferred meal plan, allergies or dietary needs, and whether some families need different arrangements.

Before accepting a quotation

Ask for the proposed room types and occupancy to be written into the quotation. Connecting rooms, extra beds and accessibility features should be confirmed by the accommodation provider, not assumed from general photographs.
