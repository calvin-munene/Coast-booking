Why Diani works for groups

Diani is known for white sand beaches, a relaxed setting and a premium coastal feel. It can suit company retreats, church retreats, family reunions, university groups and tours that want a more resort-style coastal experience.

Planning your Diani stay

Diani is south of Mombasa, so transport and arrival timing matter. If your group needs transfers, special meal planning or nearby activity access, include those details in your request. We help you clarify requirements before confirming an arrangement.
