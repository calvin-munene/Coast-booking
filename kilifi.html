A calm coast option for organized groups

Kilifi is often preferred by groups looking for a quieter environment than central Mombasa. It can work for training programs, field teams, retreats and education-related travel where calm surroundings and coordinated meals matter.

Submit your group details once and Coast Bookings will review suitable accommodation possibilities. We do not show public prices because availability and suitability depend on dates, rooming, meals and group size.

Compare with Watamu and Mombasa

Kilifi can be ideal for a slower coast experience. If your group wants more tourism activity, Watamu may also work. If you need airport and city access, Mombasa may be better.
