Prepare an accommodation brief for a training group

For NGO workshops and field teams, a clear brief makes procurement easier. Separate overnight guests from day participants, and distinguish a residential workshop from an itinerary that moves between fieldwork locations.

Include these details in your enquiry

  • Workshop dates, overnight guest count and any day delegates requiring meals or meeting space.
  • Training-room layout, equipment, internet access and the hours the room is needed.
  • Participant accessibility requirements, dietary needs and any separate facilitator rooms.
  • Organization billing details and required quotation or invoice information; do not send sensitive participant documents in an initial enquiry.

Before accepting a quotation

Request a breakdown of rooms, meals, meeting space and any other services. Your organization should confirm its own procurement, safeguarding and payment requirements before accepting an option.
