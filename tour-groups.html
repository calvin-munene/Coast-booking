Turn the tour itinerary into a clear rooming request

A multi-stop tour needs more detail than a single hotel check-in. Send the overnight locations and dates together with a rooming breakdown so a proposed stay can be checked against the rest of the itinerary.

Include these details in your enquiry

  • Night-by-night dates and preferred destinations, plus any flexibility to consider another coastal area.
  • Twin, double and single-room requirements, including any separate guide or driver rooms.
  • Arrival point, expected arrival time and departure plans for each stop.
  • Meals needed at each property, dietary requests and any early breakfast or packed-meal requirement.

Before accepting a quotation

Ask which services the quotation includes, and confirm rooming-change deadlines and cancellation conditions before accepting. Excursions, guides, attraction admission and transfers are not automatically part of accommodation.
