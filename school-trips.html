Prepare the school accommodation request

A useful school-trip brief separates learner numbers from accompanying staff and identifies the supervision and rooming arrangements the school needs. This helps avoid comparing quotations based on different assumptions.

Include these details in your enquiry

  • Learner and staff numbers, age ranges and separate staff-room requirements.
  • Travel dates, planned arrival time and room-sharing requirements set by the school.
  • Meal times, dietary requirements, allergies and any request for packed meals.
  • Visit itinerary, transport plans, accessibility needs and the school’s supervision arrangements.

Before accepting a quotation

The school should confirm permissions, safeguarding, supervision, emergency procedures and attraction access separately. Ask for room types, occupancy, meal plan, payment and cancellation conditions in writing before accepting an option.
