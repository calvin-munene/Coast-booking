What to confirm for a Diani group stay

Begin with the purpose of your stay: a family gathering, retreat and working team visit can need different room layouts and meal schedules even when the group size is the same.

Include these details in your enquiry

  • Adults, children and staff or organizers, with the required room-sharing arrangement.
  • Travel dates, arrival point and expected check-in time so transfers can be discussed separately.
  • Meals required for each night, dietary needs and any activities that affect the daily schedule.
  • Meeting space, accessibility or other facilities that must be confirmed by the accommodation provider.

Before accepting a quotation

Ask for the property name, proposed room types, meal plan and total inclusions in your quotation. Photos of Diani Beach show the destination, not a guarantee of beachfront rooms or a particular hotel.
