Match the team stay to the fixture schedule

Start with the competition venue and fixture times, not only a preferred beach area. A practical rooming and meal schedule can matter more than proximity to leisure activities when a squad has early starts or late finishes.

Include these details in your enquiry

  • Players, coaches and support-staff numbers, with a separate rooming list for each group.
  • Fixture or training venue names and the planned arrival and departure schedule.
  • Early breakfasts, packed-meal requests, hydration needs and dietary requirements that must be quoted.
  • Equipment storage, laundry or recovery-space requests, if required; each must be explicitly confirmed.

Before accepting a quotation

Confirm check-in and check-out times, transport arrangements and responsibility for equipment. Accommodation does not automatically include training facilities, venue access or transfers.
