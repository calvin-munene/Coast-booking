Organize accommodation around the learning itinerary

For a field visit, the academic schedule should guide the location choice. Share the places your group needs to visit and the number of nights in each area before comparing rooms along the coast.

Include these details in your enquiry

  • Student and accompanying-staff numbers, with separate staff-room requirements.
  • Fieldwork locations, visit dates and any early starts that affect meals or travel.
  • Room-sharing preferences, accessibility needs and the institution’s supervision arrangements.
  • Meal plan, dietary requirements, and whether an organization quotation or invoice is required.

Before accepting a quotation

The institution should arrange and verify permissions, supervision and access to study sites. Admission charges, guides, fieldwork access and transport are separate unless included in the written quotation.
