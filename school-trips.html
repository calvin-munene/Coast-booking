School Group Accommodation

Coast Bookings coordinates school group accommodation for educational trips, student tours and coastal programs in Mombasa, Nyali, Bamburi, Shanzu, Diani, Kilifi and Watamu.

Request a Group Quote

What schools usually need

School trips require safe, practical and organized accommodation. Coordinators often need separate rooming plans, staff rooms, full-board meals, early breakfasts, transport notes and proximity to planned activities.

Instead of public rates, Coast Bookings asks for your student count, staff count, dates, destination and meal needs so suitable options can be reviewed properly.

Best destinations

  • Mombasa for easy transport and attractions.
  • Nyali and Bamburi for North Coast access.
  • Diani for a premium South Coast trip.
  • Watamu or Kilifi for scenic educational travel.

School trip FAQ

Can meals be included?

Yes. You can request bed and breakfast, half board, full board or special diet support.

Do you own school dormitories?

No. Coast Bookings coordinates with trusted accommodation partners based on each school's requirements.
