Choose a Mombasa base around your itinerary

Mombasa group enquiries can involve the city and several different North Coast areas. Share the places you need to reach, rather than assuming every accommodation option has the same access to your activities.

Include these details in your enquiry

  • Main visit, meeting or fixture locations, plus arrival and departure points.
  • Dates and guest numbers, with adults, children or accompanying staff listed separately.
  • Room-sharing requirements, preferred meals and any accessibility needs.
  • Any requested meeting space or transport, marked separately from the accommodation requirement.

Before accepting a quotation

Compare the actual proposed location as well as room and meal arrangements. Admission to Fort Jesus or other attractions, guides and transfers are separate unless the written quotation includes them.
